Nalarasa Ops AI

AI-assisted operational toolkit for hospitality workflows, SOP automation, training systems, and SME productivity tools.

---

Overview

Nalarasa Ops AI is an open source operational toolkit designed to help hospitality businesses, restaurants, cafes, and SMEs improve workflow consistency, staff productivity, operational standardization, and training accessibility using AI-assisted systems.

The project combines operational knowledge, hospitality workflow logic, and practical AI tooling into accessible automation utilities that support real-world business operations.
